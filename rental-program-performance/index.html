Why this matters

Occupancy alone does not tell the whole story. Lowering nightly rates can increase occupancy without necessarily increasing owner revenue. For that reason, occupancy, average nightly rate and revenue per available night should be evaluated together to understand how effectively Seaspray converts local tourism demand into rental revenue for owners.

Default Seaspray inputs come from the 2025 rotation report: 91 units, 11,129 rental nights and about $238.75 of reported revenue per rental night. The default 33,215 available-night figure is only 91 units × 365 calendar nights. Replace it with true bookable nights when available.
