A Clear and Respectful Response

What Owners First Means for Seaspray

David Burwell's recent election email questioned the purpose of the Owners First campaign, the substance of the concerns owners have shared, and our use of artificial intelligence as a communication tool. We respect David's years of service and his right to make the case for the current Board.

We also want owners to hear directly from us. This is not about personalities or creating a back-and-forth argument. It is about the relationship owners should have with the people they elect, the level of transparency they should expect, and the standard of service Seaspray should provide to owners and guests.

We are not trying to take over or transform the Seaspray we all know and love. We are asking owners to help usher in a season of more open communication, greater transparency, responsible use of technology, stronger customer service, and a Board that consistently remembers whom it was elected to serve.

Our guiding belief is simple. Board members are not above the owners. They are elected by the owners, accountable to the owners, and entrusted to serve the owners.

Year-Round Representation

Owners Should Be Heard Between Elections

For many owners, the most important issue is not whether they receive campaign messages during an election. It is whether they hear from, and feel heard by, their elected representatives throughout the year.

That is especially important when the Board is considering issues that may affect owners financially, legally, operationally, or personally. Owners should receive timely information, understand the choices being considered, and have a meaningful opportunity to share their views before major decisions are made.

Communication should be a year-round responsibility, not an election-season activity.

Owner Rights

Transparency Should Go Beyond Minimum Compliance

Florida condominium law requires access to official records, properly noticed meetings, Board meetings that are open to owners in most circumstances, and opportunities for owners to speak and ask questions. It also states that directors have a fiduciary relationship to unit owners. These protections establish an important baseline, but Owners First does not believe the legal minimum should become Seaspray's standard.

Posting required documents in a shared online repository and restricting owners to a two-minute comment period under an Association meeting rule may satisfy a technical obligation. It does not necessarily create informed owners, meaningful participation, or open communication. Florida law permits associations to adopt reasonable rules governing the duration and manner of owner comments; it does not itself require Seaspray to limit every owner to two minutes.

Florida's increasingly detailed transparency and participation requirements reflect the serious risks that arise when owners lack access to information or a meaningful voice. Owners First believes Seaspray should be known as a model of openness and communication, not simply an organization that does the minimum required.

Many of us think of Seaspray as more than a property. It is a close community with the warmth and character of Old Florida. Our hope is to restore more of that sense of connection by creating an environment where owners know one another, receive important information, and feel comfortable participating throughout the year.

Compliance is the floor. Seaspray should set the standard for transparency, communication, and owner participation.

Informed Trust

Owners Should Not Have to Rely on Blind Trust

Owners should not simply be asked to trust that Board members are being open, honest, and guided by the owners' best interests. With consistent communication and meaningful transparency, owners can see what is happening, understand why decisions are being made, and determine for themselves whether their interests are being protected.

We expect Seaspray owners to demand a great deal from the people they elect. All three Owners First candidates are committed to working hard for every owner, being honest about challenges facing the Association, and actively inviting owners to share their questions, concerns, and ideas.

Trust is strongest when it is supported by information, openness, and accountability.

Responsible Innovation

We Embrace AI and Other Tools That Help Us Serve Owners Better

Some may view the use of artificial intelligence as a negative. We do not. As Board candidates, we believe responsible leaders should use available technology when it helps them work more efficiently, communicate more clearly, reduce costs, analyze information, or compete more effectively.

AI-assisted tools are already common across medical facilities, universities, banking and financial institutions, automobile manufacturers, government agencies, travel companies, digital advertising platforms, and vacation-rental competitors throughout the Fort Walton Beach market. AI is also embedded in many modern website and software platforms and in Google's advertising products, making it relevant to Seaspray's own new website, software, and much-discussed Google advertising campaign.

David's email referenced our use of artificial intelligence, and we are comfortable addressing that directly. AI did not create our message or determine what we believe. Every position, priority, concern, and commitment published by this campaign was developed, reviewed, and approved by Eric, Roberto, and Zachary. Technology helped us organize our work and present it in a clear, professional, and shareable format.

Owners can expect an Owners First Board to thoughtfully embrace technologies that help us communicate better, operate more efficiently, reduce unnecessary costs, serve owners and guests more effectively, and compete to win in a highly competitive vacation-rental market.

Technology is a tool. Leadership remains responsible for how it is used and for every decision that follows.

Experience + Renewal

Experience Matters, but Knowledge Should Not Be Concentrated

We agree that prior Board service provides knowledge about the Association's operations that most owners do not have and, frankly, many owners do not want or need. Owners should not have to become full-time Association experts to understand the issues that affect their property and investment. At the same time, they should never feel that only a small group knows what is happening while everyone else remains dependent on that group for information.

Florida law generally limits condominium directors to eight consecutive years, subject to stated exceptions. The structure of that law allows both periodic renewal and continued service when owners strongly support it or too few candidates are available. One risk of allowing information to remain concentrated is that owners may not recognize an emerging problem until it has become larger, more expensive, or harder to correct.

Continuity at Seaspray is already protected through staggered terms. If all three Owners First candidates are elected, two current Board members will remain and continue serving. New members will therefore work alongside experienced members rather than replacing the entire Board at once. Florida law does not treat prior Board service as a prerequisite for serving. Elections and staggered terms allow owners to balance experience, accountability, and new perspectives.

Healthy continuity comes from documented knowledge, staggered terms, and experienced members working alongside new representatives.

Meaningful Improvement

What We Mean When We Talk About Change

Eric, Roberto, and Zachary each experienced interactions with current management or the Board that fell well short of what we believe owners should expect from those entrusted to serve them. As we spoke with other owners, we learned that our experiences were not isolated. Owners First was born from a shared determination to do everything we could to help ensure that no other owner feels dismissed, unheard, or treated inconsistently.

We also recognize that some owners have had only positive experiences and may be surprised by what others have encountered. Our goal is not to diminish those positive experiences. It is to make them the consistent experience of every owner. No owner or guest should leave an interaction with Seaspray feeling unwelcome, disregarded, or subjected to a different standard than someone else.

This is not a takeover, and it is not an effort to change the character of the Seaspray we all know and love. It is a commitment to greater transparency, more cooperative communication, consistent follow-through, meaningful owner participation, and customer service comparable to the most respected organizations in the travel and leisure industry.

Preserve what makes Seaspray special while making excellent treatment and service the experience everyone can expect.

Service Is Marketing

Every Guest Experience Becomes Part of Our Marketing

Seaspray's location, property, and individually owned condominiums give us an incredible foundation. Pairing that foundation with world-class service can turn guests into our strongest marketing force. Delighted guests tell friends, family, and coworkers, return for future vacations, leave positive reviews, and share their experiences online.

The opposite is also true. A guest who feels unwelcome, overly controlled, or disappointed may share that experience just as widely. No advertising budget can fully overcome repeated negative experiences. Successful marketing must bring guests to Seaspray, and exceptional service must give them a reason to return and recommend it.

Advertising creates interest. Outstanding experiences create loyal guests and lasting advocates.

Privacy + Transparency

Protecting Confidentiality and Informing Owners Can Coexist

We agree that confidential legal advice, privileged strategy, personnel matters, and private owner information must be protected. A central part of putting owners first is protecting them, and that responsibility certainly includes safeguarding sensitive legal, financial, medical, and personal information.

We also believe confidentiality should not be used as a blanket reason to keep owners unaware of important matters. Owners should receive appropriate general information about legal, financial, operational, or property issues that have a reasonable chance of affecting them. A responsible Board can acknowledge that an issue exists, describe its status and potential impact when disclosure is legally permitted and prudent, and invite owner feedback without revealing privileged details or compromising the Association's position.

Privacy and responsible transparency are not opposing principles. A careful Board can respect both.

“A Board should never ask owners to remain in the dark and simply trust. It should communicate in a way that earns their trust.”

Owners First is a commitment to listen throughout the year, share appropriate information, welcome owner participation, use modern tools responsibly, and make decisions with every owner in mind.
