Fresh Leadership Doesn’t Mean Replacing All 5 Board Members

Three of Seaspray’s five Board seats are up for election this year. If all three Owners First candidates are elected, Joe Stults and Jim Gebhardt will still remain on the Board and continue serving out the remainder of their terms, providing valuable continuity and experience. Eric, Roberto, and Zachary look forward to the opportunity to work collaboratively with both of them, build on what is working well, and bring fresh perspectives and ideas to help make Seaspray better for all owners.

A new majority does not mean starting over, and it does not mean all institutional knowledge is lost. It means owners are electing three new Board members with fresh perspectives, different professional backgrounds, new ideas, and most importantly, a willingness to serve and place all owners first when Board decisions are made.

A new majority brings fresh perspectives, not a loss of continuity.

Are New Candidates Qualified?

Yes. Eric Stensrud, Roberto Giacomelli, and Zachary Moody each bring extensive experience in business leadership, operations, budgeting, management, problem solving, customer relationships, and working within changing environments.

Florida condominium law is one of the many things that is important for an association like Seaspray, and it is continually changing and evolving. That is exactly why Florida law requires Board members to receive ongoing training. Eric, Roberto, and Zachary will treat this no differently than they treat anything else in their professional lives: they have already begun learning about the appropriate rules and regulations, will continue learning and staying informed on all matters relevant to Seaspray, and will rely on the experts and professionals available to them for guidance on difficult issues rather than assuming they always know what is right or best.

The important thing is not that every Board member is an expert in Florida statute. It is that, as leaders and members of the Board, they are well-rounded and skilled in many areas and know how to seek expert guidance and ask the right questions when needed, while always approaching difficult situations from an Owners First perspective.

The right questions. The right experts. A stronger Seaspray.

So What Would Be Different?

For the most part, Seaspray and the Board would continue to operate much as they always have. The difference is that they would do so better, with an Owners First mindset guiding every decision.

That means more transparency, more accountability, higher customer service, and better follow-through. Owners should not have to wonder what is happening, chase down answers, or feel like their concerns come second to the convenience of the Board or management.

We are not running to reinvent Seaspray. We are running to communicate better, listen better, manage better, and push for better results while always asking what is best for all owners.

For the most part, Seaspray would operate the same. Just better.

“Different isn’t always better, but better is always different.”

We, as the Owners First candidates, aim to be better, and that means being willing to make thoughtful changes that put owners first.
