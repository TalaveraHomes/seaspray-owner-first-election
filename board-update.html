Regarding “UPDATE on Seaspray by Board VP”

The recent update from the current Board VP, sent after the launch of our Owners First campaign, highlights the experience of the current Board and provides updates on current projects at Seaspray.

Fresh Leadership Doesn’t Mean Replacing All 5 Board Members

Three of Seaspray’s five Board seats are up for election this year. That means if all three Owners First candidates are elected, two members of the existing Board, Joe Stults and Jim Gebhardt, will remain. All three of us are excited to work with the two of them to help make Seaspray better for all owners.

A new majority does not mean starting over, and it does not mean all institutional knowledge is lost. It means owners are electing three new Board members with fresh perspectives, different professional backgrounds, new ideas, and most importantly, a willingness to serve and place all owners first when Board decisions are made.

A new majority brings fresh perspectives, not a loss of continuity.
◆

Are New Candidates Qualified?

Yes. Eric H. Stensrud, Roberto Giacomelli, and Zachary Moody each bring extensive experience in business leadership, operations, budgeting, management, problem solving, customer relationships, and working within changing environments.

Florida condominium law is important for an association like Seaspray and is continually evolving. Florida law requires education for newly elected or appointed residential condominium directors and ongoing continuing education. Eric, Roberto, and Zachary will do what they do every day in their professional lives: continue learning, stay informed on matters relevant to Seaspray, and rely on the experts and professionals available to them for guidance on difficult issues.

The important quality is not that every Board member is personally an expert in every Florida statute. It is that they know how to seek expert guidance, ask the right questions, and approach difficult situations from an Owners First perspective while looking out for the interests of all owners.

So What Would Be Different?

The Board will still manage the Association on behalf of owners and address problems and issues as they arise. The difference is that we will do so while constantly asking what is best for all owners at Seaspray.

We will be more transparent. We will demand not only results, but also a higher level of customer service for owners and guests. We believe owners deserve respect, proactive communication, accountability, and a Board that consistently places their interests first.

More transparency. Greater accountability. A Board that puts owners first.

“Different isn’t always better, but better is always different.”

We, as the Owners First candidates, aim to be better, and that means being willing to make thoughtful changes that put owners first.
